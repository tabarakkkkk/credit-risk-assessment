# Debt-to-Income Ratio Policy

## Section 1 — Standard DTI range
Debt-to-income (DTI) compares recurring monthly debt payments with gross monthly income. A DTI below 36% is within the standard review range in this educational policy bank.

## Section 2 — Elevated DTI
A DTI from 36% through 50% requires additional review of income stability, requested amount, and repayment capacity. A value above 50% is outside the standard range and should trigger a request for more information or a lower hypothetical amount.

## Source
CreditLens Policy Bank Sample. The definition is consistent with consumer education from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/.
