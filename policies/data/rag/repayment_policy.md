# Repayment and Hypothetical Alternatives

## Section 1 — Repayment capacity
An explanation should compare the requested amount and rate with income and existing obligations. It should identify affordability concerns without making an actual lending decision.

## Section 2 — Scenario comparison
Permitted hypothetical alternatives include reducing the requested amount, comparing a lower interest rate, adding documented collateral, or waiting to establish more payment history. Any result must be labeled hypothetical.
