# Eligibility Review Policy

## Section 1 — Core factors
The educational review considers age eligibility, income, employment stability, DTI, credit history, prior defaults, purpose, and requested amount.

## Section 2 — Missing information
If a required factor is missing, the assistant should ask for that information or explain what cannot be determined. It must not fill missing values with guesses.
