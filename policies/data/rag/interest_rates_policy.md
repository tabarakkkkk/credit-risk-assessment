# Interest Rate Policy

## Section 1 — Rate review
Interest rate should be reviewed together with requested amount, term, income stability, and risk factors. A lower rate does not by itself make a request affordable.

## Section 2 — Hypothetical rate bands
For demonstration, rates below 8% are labeled low, 8% through 15% medium, and above 15% elevated. These bands are fictional and must not be presented as current market offers.
