# Required Documents Policy

## Section 1 — Identity and income
Typical review materials may include identity evidence, recent income evidence, employment information, and permission to verify relevant information.

## Section 2 — Loan-specific evidence
Depending on purpose, the reviewer may request tuition information, medical estimates, contractor estimates, business plans, or evidence for debt consolidation. The exact list depends on the lender and jurisdiction.
