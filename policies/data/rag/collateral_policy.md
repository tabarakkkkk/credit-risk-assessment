# Collateral Policy

## Section 1 — Purpose of collateral
Collateral is an asset that may support a secured loan. The reviewer should record the asset type, estimated value, ownership, and any existing claims.

## Section 2 — Collateral coverage
Collateral value should not be treated as guaranteed recovery. A hypothetical review can compare the requested amount with the documented value, while clearly stating that valuation and legal review are still required.
