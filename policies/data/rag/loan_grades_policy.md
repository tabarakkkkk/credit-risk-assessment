# Loan Grade Policy

## Section 1 — Grade A and B
Grade A describes a hypothetical lower-risk profile with stable income, manageable DTI, and consistent credit history. Grade B describes a profile that may need additional evidence but remains within a moderate review range.

## Section 2 — Grade C and D
Grade C indicates elevated uncertainty or risk factors. Grade D indicates that the request requires significant additional review. Grades are explanatory labels, not approval decisions.
