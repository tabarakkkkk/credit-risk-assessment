# Loan Amount Limits by Purpose

## Section 1 — Educational loans
For this demo policy bank, an education request may be compared against a hypothetical limit of 50,000 currency units. The limit is illustrative and is not a real lender rule.

## Section 2 — Medical and personal loans
Medical requests may use a hypothetical limit of 35,000 units, while personal requests may use 25,000 units. The assistant should explain that purpose-specific limits require lender policy confirmation.

## Section 3 — Home improvement and venture
Home-improvement requests may use 75,000 units and venture requests 100,000 units in this demo. These values are synthetic examples for retrieval testing.
