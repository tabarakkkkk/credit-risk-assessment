# Credit History Policy

## Section 1 — Minimum history
The reviewer should check the age of the applicant's credit history, the number of active accounts, and whether payments were made on time. A longer, consistent history provides more evidence than a short history.

## Section 2 — Limited history
When history is limited, the assistant should identify the missing evidence and suggest a smaller hypothetical amount or additional documentation. It must not invent a score or approve an application.

## Source
CreditLens Policy Bank Sample, informed by general consumer credit-report education from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.
